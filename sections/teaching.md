### Teaching Experience

#### University of West Georgia — XIDS 2002: College Ethics
*Fall 2019 - Fall 2020, in-person and hybrid*

This course examines ethical questions and situations that affect college students in the American higher education system. Topics include affirmative action, free speech & campus protest, friendship & dating in college, sexual misconduct, the costs of higher education, financial aid, plagiarism, performance-enhancing drugs, extra credit and the aims of higher education.
