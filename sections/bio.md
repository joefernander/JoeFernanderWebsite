### Bio

I work at the intersection of learning analytics, educational research, psychometrics, survey methodology, and data science. In my current role, I serve as Assistant Dean for Digital Innovation and Data Scientist at the University System of Georgia [eCampus](https://www.ecampus.usg.edu "USG eCampus provides high quality and affordable online learning experiences to all public colleges & universities in the State of Georgia."), where I lead our Data Science and Salesforce teams. My primary interests focus on effectively using learner data to improve educational outcomes, predict student behaviors, and increase the efficiency of our operations and overall learner experience. I am also passionate about designing and analyzing surveys to better understand student sentiment and their experience in our learning environments.

The Data Science Team primarily uses [Posit Team](https://posit.co/products/enterprise/team/ "I <3 Posit!") for developing R, Quarto, Shiny applications, and Plumber to design data applications, deliver insights, and host our own APIs. Like many data scientists, I find myself also serving as a data engineer. In this role, I built and maintain MS SQL and PostgreSQL databases with engineering pipelines developed and deployed using Quarto and Posit Connect.

### Research Interests

- Using learner data to influence student learning outcomes (through quality enhancements and predictive analytics)
- Educational design-based research in online learning environments
- Interventions for increasing self-directed learning, academic self-concept, and mindset
- Using large language models (LLMs) for academic tutoring and personalized learning
- Survey methodologies for understanding student perceptions and sentiments
