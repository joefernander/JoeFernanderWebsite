### Bio

I work at the intersection of learning analytics, learning technologies, applied educational psychology, survey methodology, and data science. I serve as Assistant Dean for Digital Innovation and Data Scientist at the University System of Georgia [eCampus](https://www.ecampus.usg.edu), where I lead our Data Science and Salesforce teams. I am also a doctoral student in Learning Sciences and Learning Technologies at Georgia State University. My work centers on using learner data to improve educational outcomes, predict student behavior, and make our operations and learner experience more efficient. I'm especially interested in helping students adopt and sustain effective study strategies, such as retrieval practice and spaced learning, and in how evidence-based interventions and AI-driven tutoring tools can support them in becoming more strategic, motivated, and self-regulated learners. I am also passionate about designing and analyzing surveys that help us understand how students perceive and experience our learning environments.

Our Data Science team builds on [Posit Team](https://posit.co/products/enterprise/team/), using R, Quarto, Shiny, and Plumber to develop data applications, deliver insights, and host our own APIs. Like many data scientists, I also serve as a data engineer: I build and maintain our MS SQL and PostgreSQL databases, along with data pipelines developed in Quarto and deployed on Posit Connect.

### Research Interests

- Using learner data to improve student learning outcomes through continuous improvement and predictive modeling

- Interventions that promote the adoption and sustained use of effective study strategies

- Applied interventions that foster growth mindset, self-regulated learning, and expectancy-value beliefs

- Machine learning and large language models (LLMs) for personalized tutoring and study support

- Survey methods for measuring student perceptions, beliefs, and experiences
